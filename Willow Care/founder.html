Why I built Willow

It started with my brother.

"They told us he'd never speak, or live a normal life. My family refused to accept that."

— The reason Willow exists

My younger brother has autism, and he's nonverbal. When he was little, his doctors were blunt: he would never talk, and he'd never live what they called a "normal" life. We didn't argue with them. We just quietly decided not to accept it.

So we showed up, every day, for years. Therapies, routines, small experiments, endless patience. And slowly, he grew. He's come so much further than anyone predicted, and watching that happen changed how I see what's possible for anyone.

But I also saw what slipped through the cracks. Notes on paper, updates buried in group texts, details that lived only in one person's memory. We were doing so much right, and still missing patterns that were sitting right in front of us. I kept thinking: if we could just see it all in one place, we could help him even faster.

That's Willow. A calm, shared home for a care team to track the whole picture, so the next family that refuses to accept "no" starts with better tools than we had.

The human part

A few things about me.

When I'm not building Willow, I'm usually building something else — and roping other people into it.

I build things from scratch

I love understanding how things actually work, so I make my own; little neural networks, ciphers, and tools that run right in the browser. No shortcuts, just curiosity.

I move fast when it matters

Give me a deadline and I'll surprise you. I once built a working robot — and the app to drive it — in about a week for an education nonprofit.

I love teaching

I started my school's cybersecurity club to get classmates hands-on with real tools. Passing on what I know is honestly my favorite part of any project.

Family comes first

Everything I make traces back to the people I love. Willow most of all — it's the project my whole life kind of pointed toward.

From my family to yours

Willow is for your family, too.

If you're coordinating care for someone you love, I'd be honored to have Willow help carry some of it.
