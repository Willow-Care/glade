What Willow does

Four pillars. One source of truth.

Willow gives every care team everything it needs to stay aligned, from the first morning check-in to the last note before lights out.

01

Central care feed

A continuous, chronological timeline of meals, mood, therapy sessions, and daily observations.

  • Real-time log updates
  • Automated shift handover summaries
02

Medication & vitals

Precise adherence logs and biometric dashboards that surface trends before they become emergencies.

  • Dosage & adherence tracking
  • Visual vitals charts over time
03

Smart care calendar

One shared calendar for appointments, shifts, family visits, and therapy, plus clear task ownership.

  • Unified shared scheduling
  • Assignable daily task checklists
04

Access & permissions

Secure, multi-tenant data isolation so every family's information stays inside their own network.

  • Family admins get full control
  • Aides get scoped daily-log access
Willow in practice

Scroll through a day inside Willow.

Feed, logs, vitals, behavior tracking, tasks, and AI insights — the major features, one scroll at a time. Real product screenshots are the fastest way to earn a family's trust.

  1. 01 · Live feed

    Every update, in one timeline

    Meals, mood, medications, therapy notes — the whole day flows into a single chronological feed the entire care team sees in real time.

  2. 02 · Daily logs

    Capture the whole day in seconds

    A fast, structured log for mood, sleep, medications, meals, and time breakdown — with drafts so a busy shift can finish later.

  3. 03 · Vitals & trends

    Spot trends before they become emergencies

    Heart rate, blood pressure, weight, and oxygen — logged over time and charted so subtle changes surface early.

  4. 04 · Behavior log

    Structured behavior tracking

    Record incidents with clinical ABC structure — antecedent, behavior, consequence, and intensity — ready to share with a BCBA.

  5. 05 · Tasks & calendar

    Shared schedule, clear ownership

    Board, list, and calendar views for appointments, routines, and daily checklists — every task assigned to a real person.

  6. 06 · AI insights

    A BCBA-style read on the week

    Willow analyzes recent logs like a compassionate analyst — surfacing mood/sleep correlations, patterns, and gentle, actionable suggestions.
